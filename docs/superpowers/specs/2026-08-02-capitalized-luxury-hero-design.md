# Capitalized Luxury Hero Design

## Goal

Make the storefront feel more premium by restoring natural capitalization and replacing the empty-looking hero bottles.

## Approved direction

- Render the wordmark as `Jardin Secret`, retaining its stacked editorial serif treatment.
- Present every catalog brand and fragrance name in Title Case, including supplier records that arrive fully lowercase.
- Replace the hero artwork with a portrait editorial still life of visibly filled amber, blush and smoky-green perfume bottles in heavy glass with refined metallic caps.
- Keep the existing light sage palette, arched crop, hero layout, links and responsive behavior.
- The artwork must contain no labels, logos, packaging, text or watermarks.

## Verification

Component tests assert the capitalized wordmark, untransformed product typography and new hero asset. A production build plus desktop and mobile browser checks verify rendering, image loading and overflow.
